Scientific Notes

Radiation quantities and units describe different physical concepts.

Sievert (Sv): equivalent or effective dose, expressed with radiation and tissue weighting considerations.

Gray (Gy): absorbed energy per unit mass. One gray equals one joule per kilogram.

Becquerel (Bq): radioactive activity. One becquerel equals one nuclear transformation per second.

Dose rate: dose delivered per unit time. Accumulated dose is estimated by multiplying a constant rate by the exposure duration.

Gy and Sv are not interchangeable by a simple unit-prefix conversion. Their relationship depends on radiation type, geometry and dosimetric quantity.
